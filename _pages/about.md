I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the [School of Computing](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/), [National University of Singapore](https://www.nus.edu.sg/), under the supervision of Prof. [Gim Hee Lee](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~leegh/).

I received my M.S. in Electronic Information from the [Multi-spectral Visual Information Processing Group](https://mvp.whu.edu.cn/index.htm), [Electronic Information School](http://eis.whu.edu.cn/), [Wuhan University](https://www.whu.edu.cn/) in 2026, fortunately working with Prof. [Jiayi Ma](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=73trMQkAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao) and Prof. [Yong Ma](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=x3Q8PHIAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate). Before that, I received my B.E. from [Central South University](https://www.csu.edu.cn/) in 2024.

I am broadly interested in Embodied AI and 3D vision. During my Master's, I worked on 3D shape analysis, with a focus on non-rigid shape matching. Currently, I am exploring vision-language-action (VLA) models and world models, aiming to build embodied agents that can perceive, understand, and interact with the physical world.

If you would like to collaborate, please feel free to contact me.
