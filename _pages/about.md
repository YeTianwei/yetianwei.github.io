I am Tianwei Ye, a master student at [Wuhan University](https://www.whu.edu.cn/) under the supervision of Prof. [Jiayi Ma](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=73trMQkAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao) and Prof. [Yong Ma](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=x3Q8PHIAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate) in the [Multi-spectral Visual information Processing group](https://mvp.whu.edu.cn/index.htm). Before that I received my Bechelor's degree from [Central South University](https://www.csu.edu.cn/) in 2024.

I have a broad interest in computer vision and graphics, particularly in 3D vision. I am now working on 3D shape matching with deep learning and optimization techniques. If you have interest to collaborate with me, feel free to contact me.

Currently, I'm seeking PhD opportunities with a focus on Computer Vision.
